Fasting Calendar

Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Fasting Calendar is made by QuickQuote Group PTY LTD, an Australian company ("we", "us"). In short: everything you enter stays on your phone, we never receive it, and there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your tradition, reminder times, the foods your priest allows you, and the fasts you tick off are stored only on your device (and in your own iPhone backups). The calendar is worked out on your phone, so the app doesn't need the internet to show it. We never receive any of it.

Purchases

Fasting Calendar Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment and tells the app whether Pro is active. We don't receive your name, email or payment details.

What we don't do

We don't collect, sell or share your information, show ads, use analytics, or track you across other apps and websites.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. If you have a question or complaint, email us. If you're in Australia you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner; if you're in the EU or UK, your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above.
