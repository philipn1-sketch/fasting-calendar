Help and support

Questions, problems or a date that looks wrong for your parish? Email admin@quick-quote.com.au and we'll get back to you.

Choosing your tradition

On first launch pick the calendar your parish keeps: Orthodox New Calendar (the Nativity on 25 December), Orthodox Old Calendar (the Nativity on 7 January) or Catholic. Change it any time in Settings.

Today and the calendar

The home screen shows whether today is a fast day, the fast or feast, and whether meat, dairy and eggs, fish, wine and oil are allowed. "Some allow" means practice differs, so follow your parish. Tap any day on the calendar to see it. Darker days are stricter; a gold dot marks a feast.

Reminders

Turn on the morning reminder in Settings to be told on fast days what is allowed. With Pro you also get a heads up 3 days before Great Lent, the Nativity, Apostles' and Dormition Fasts, or Ash Wednesday.

Fasting Calendar Pro

Pro adds Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, season alerts, "My priest allows me" (foods your priest has relaxed show as allowed), and a log of the fasts you keep with a streak. Restore a purchase or manage your subscription in Settings.

Is the calendar right?

It's worked out from the date of Pascha or Easter each year using common rules. Local practice varies, so it is a general guide. If your parish differs, follow your parish, and let us know.
